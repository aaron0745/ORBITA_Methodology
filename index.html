Project Overview & Motivation

Bridging the Gap Between Passive Coolers and High-Cost Perfusion Machines

Organ transplantation is a time-critical process in which donated organs must be preserved under strict, controlled environmental conditions during transit between donor and recipient hospitals.

In many regions, including India, organs are predominantly transported using conventional ice-filled hard cooler boxes. While economical, these passive boxes suffer from major limitations:

  • Zero Real-Time Observability: Doctors have no visibility into internal temperatures or handling anomalies during transit.
  • No Automated Regulation: Melting ice leads to uncontrolled thermal warming ($>8^\circ\text{C}$) or direct frostbite damage ($<0^\circ\text{C}$).
  • Undetected Mechanical Trauma: Potholes, drops, and sudden acceleration induce shearing stress in cellular vasculature.
  • Cost Barrier of Existing Alternatives: Commercial hypothermic machine perfusion devices (e.g., TransMedics OCS, OrganOx) cost upwards of $40,000 to $80,000, rendering them unattainable for the vast majority of hospitals.

Data Engineering & Training

Dataset Acquisition Strategy

Real human organ transit logs are legally classified and unavailable in public repositories. We formulate a robust 3-tier hybrid data pipeline combining empirical physical trials and thermodynamic simulation.

01

Hardware-in-the-Loop Logging

Deploy the assembled prototype in simulated cold runs with thermal surrogates (gel ice packs, cold 0.9% saline bags, or biological tissue samples):

  • ▶ Nominal Transit: Steady 2°C–6°C on highway ambulance trips.
  • ▶ Thermal Failure: Peltier turned off in 38°C ambient room to log thermal decay curve.
  • ▶ Handling Abuse: Potholes, drop tests, 5–45 second lid breaches.
02

Thermodynamic Physics Simulator

Generate 100,000+ synthetic transport trajectories in Python using Newton's cooling law and kinetic damage modeling:

$$\frac{dT_{in}}{dt} = -k(T_{in} - T_{amb}) + Q_{cooling}$$

Inject stochastic Gaussian vibration noise, Poisson-distributed road shocks ($g$-forces > 2.0g), and organ-specific decay constants to train ML classifiers.

03

Cold-Chain Logistics Datasets

Transfer learning using verified open-access biomedical cold-chain repositories:

  • ▶ WHO PQS Vaccine Cold-Chain: Real 2°C–8°C time-out-of-refrigeration excursion data.
  • ▶ Kaggle Cold Supply Chain: Multi-sensor road transport vibration and GPS telemetry.
  • ▶ UNOS/ISHLT Literature: Clinical graft loss odds ratios indexed against Cold Ischemia Time (CIT).
Mathematical Formulation

Transport Risk Index (TRI) Model

The TRI is an objective index $TRI \in [0, 100]$ synthesizing thermal degradation, organ-specific ischemic limits, kinematic shocks, and box hermetic integrity into a single predictive score.

Composite Master Equation
$$TRI(t) = \min\Big(100, \; w_1 S_{temp}(t) + w_2 S_{time}(t) + w_3 S_{shock}(t) + w_4 S_{lid}(t)\Big)$$

1. Thermal Degradation Penalty

S_temp(t)

Combines instantaneous excursion penalty with cumulative degree-minute thermal debt above optimal storage ($4^\circ\text{C}$):

$$\Delta T(t) = \begin{cases} 100, & T(t) \le 0^\circ\text{C} \quad \text{(Frostbite/Crystal)} \\ 0, & 2^\circ\text{C} \le T(t) \le 6^\circ\text{C} \quad \text{(Optimal)} \\ \left(\frac{T(t) - 6}{2}\right) \cdot 40, & 6^\circ\text{C} < T(t) \le 8^\circ\text{C} \\ 40 + 60\left(1 - e^{-0.4(T(t)-8)}\right), & T(t) > 8^\circ\text{C} \quad \text{(Warm Ischemia)} \end{cases}$$
$$S_{temp}(t) = 0.70 \cdot \Delta T(t) + 0.30 \cdot \min\left(100, \; \lambda \int_0^t \max(0, T(\tau)-6)\,d\tau\right)$$

2. Cold Ischemia Exhaustion

S_time(t)

Non-linear quadratic penalty representing ATP breakdown relative to organ maximum safe preservation ceiling ($CIT_{max}$):

$$S_{time}(t) = 100 \times \left( \frac{t_{elapsed}}{CIT_{max}} \right)^2$$
• Heart / Lung: CIT_max = 6 hours
• Liver: CIT_max = 12 hours
• Kidney: CIT_max = 30 hours

3. Mechanical Shock & Vibration

S_shock(t)

Detects peak $g$-forces above tolerance ($g_{thresh} = 2.0g$) that cause micro-vascular tearing, alongside continuous RMS road vibration:

$$S_{shock}(t) = \min\left(100, \; \mu \sum_{k=1}^{K} \max(0, g_k - 2.0)^2 + \nu \cdot \text{RMS}_{vib}\right)$$
Where $g_k = \frac{\sqrt{a_x^2 + a_y^2 + a_z^2}}{9.81\text{ m/s}^2}$.

4. Lid Tamper & Contamination

S_lid(t)

Penalizes chamber exposure to ambient pathogens and convective thermal heat rush:

$$S_{lid}(t) = \min\Big(100, \; 30 \cdot N_{open} + 0.5 \cdot t_{open\_seconds}\Big)$$
Each unauthorized opening imposes an immediate 30-point base penalty plus continuous penalty for open exposure.
